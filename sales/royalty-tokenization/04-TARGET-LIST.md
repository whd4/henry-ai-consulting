## Tier 1 — Highest fit (start here, this week)

### A. Texas mid-cap independents with mineral interests on their books
**Profile:** 5–50 wells, family-owned or founder-led, Houston / Midland / Tyler-based, decision can move fast (no committee).

**Why fit:** they own royalty + mineral interests directly. They've considered selling at some point. They want capital without losing operating control. Often have estate planning pressure (founder is 60+, kids may not want to operate).

**How to source (this week):**
- Texas RRC operator database — filter for active operators with 10–50 wells
- TIPRO member directory (Texas Independent Producers and Royalty Owners Association) — they publish a member roster
- Permian Basin Petroleum Association membership
- Texas Alliance of Energy Producers

**Outreach:** Variant B email. Use Whitt's family-name angle aggressively.

**Examples to research and verify before contacting:**
- Henry Resources (Midland) — Henry Petroleum legacy, family-owned, mid-Permian
- Chevron-divested family operators (lots in West Texas after 2014–2018 divestiture)
- Eagle Ford / Permian estate-administered interests (probate court records are public)

### B. Mineral aggregators — small-to-mid (under $200M AUM)
**Profile:** companies that buy mineral interests from individuals. They have the buy-side relationships and they have illiquid assets they could re-tokenize for partial liquidity.

**Why fit:** they understand the market. They're already a buyer of fractional interests. Tokenization gives them a new exit (sell tokens to retail) without selling whole interests.

**How to source:**
- Energy Capital Partners / NGP Energy / Quantum Energy Partners portfolio companies
- "mineral acquisition" Google searches → state-by-state lists
- LinkedIn search: "mineral buyer" + Texas

**Outreach:** Variant B with a tweak — reframe as "you're already a fractional buyer, here's a new distribution channel."

### C. Family offices with O&G holdings
**Profile:** Houston / Dallas / Tulsa family offices, $50M–$500M, with grandfather-era oil money still in the portfolio. Often hold mineral interests as legacy positions they don't actively manage.

**Why fit:** they want yield, want partial liquidity for diversification, but family politics make selling whole interests painful. Tokenization solves the politics: "we're not selling, we're modernizing the structure."

**How to source:**
- Houston FOX (Family Office Exchange) member list
- Dallas Family Office Conference attendees
- Tulsa Petroleum Club member list (legacy oil families)

**Outreach:** Variant A, with explicit estate-planning angle.

## Tier 2 — Strong fit (start week 2)

### D. Probate-administered mineral interests
**Profile:** estate executors administering mineral interests that need to be partitioned among heirs. Currently the only options are sell-or-keep — tokenization gives them fractionalize.

**How to source:** Texas probate court records (public), county-by-county. Look for estates with mineral interests > $500K. Probate attorneys are gatekeepers — find 3 probate attorneys in Houston/San Antonio/Tyler who specialize in oil & gas estates.

### E. Divorce-driven interest divisions
**Profile:** divorce attorneys handling community-property splits where mineral interests are the largest asset.

**How to source:** Houston divorce attorneys specializing in high-net-worth divorces. There's a small list — 10–15 firms.

**Note:** sensitive — pitch to the ATTORNEYS, not the parties. Attorneys love a structured solution that takes pressure off them.

## Sourcing tools

**Free / public:**
- **Texas RRC operator search**: rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/
- **Texas RRC P-4 transfer filings**: shows recent operator changes (signal: they're active in transactions)
- **County clerk records** (Harris, Midland, Reeves, Loving, Karnes): mineral deeds are public
- **TIPRO member directory** (need to be a member or a friend)

## Build a tracker (do this Monday morning, 30 minutes)

Open Google Sheets. Create columns:

| Name | Company | Title | Tier | Email | Phone | Source | Date sent v1 | Date sent v2 | Date sent v3 | Reply? | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

States for "Status":
- LEAD (added, not yet contacted)
- SENT-1 (first email out)
- SENT-2 (follow-up 1 out)
- SENT-3 (follow-up 2 out)
- REPLY-WARM (asked for more info)
- REPLY-COLD (no thanks)
- CALL-BOOKED (next step)
- PROPOSAL-OUT (formal proposal sent)
- CLOSED-WON (signed engagement)
- CLOSED-LOST (declined formally)
- DROPPED (no reply after 3 touches, parked)

Daily ritual: 9:00 AM — open sheet, send 5–10 emails, log them, move warm leads through the pipeline.
