## Engagement summary

[Client] holds [number] producing royalty interests in [Texas / specific basin / county]. This engagement produces a Tokenization Readiness Brief evaluating each interest as a potential fractional offering under the SEC Innovation Exemption (April 2026), brokers two preliminary platform conversations, and delivers a final recommendation with confidence scoring.

## Scope of work

**Week 1 — Intake + initial valuation**
- Kickoff meeting (60 min, in person or Teams)
- Data intake: production reports (last 12 months), lease documents, royalty statements, decimal interests, well-by-well status
- Initial NPV valuation per interest using HENRY proprietary models
- Risk flag report (lease integrity, operator-side concerns, regulatory exposure)

**Week 2 — Platform mapping + structure design**
- Mapping of viable tokenization platforms (Ondo, Securitize, Centrifuge, custom-rails options)
- Draft term sheet for proposed fractional offering
- Initial tax structure recommendation (coordination with [Client]'s CPA)
- Mid-week 60-min sync call

**Week 3 — Platform brokering**
- Broker introduction calls with two platforms (one Ondo-tier, one direct-to-bank)
- [Client] sits in on both calls; HENRY drives the agenda
- Compile platform feedback into structured comparison

**Week 4 — Final brief + recommendation**
- Tokenization Readiness Brief (15-20 pages, redacted version available as sample)
- Final recommendation: tokenize / wait / sell whole — with confidence score
- Final 60-min recommendation call (in person preferred)
- All work product transferred (PDFs, draft term sheets, platform contacts)

## What [Client] provides

- Production reports for last 12 months per interest
- Lease documents and royalty deeds
- Most recent royalty statement(s)
- Access to existing CPA for tax-structure coordination
- 4 hours of synchronous time across the engagement window
- Authorization to brief two tokenization platforms in industry generalities (no asset specifics without written approval)

## What HENRY provides

- All deliverables listed above
- NDA-protected working environment
- Proprietary HENRY AI infrastructure for valuation and disclosure analysis
- 48-hour response time for questions during engagement window
- 30-day post-delivery support for follow-up questions

## Deliverable inventory

1. Tokenization Readiness Brief (PDF, 15–20 pages)
2. Per-interest NPV valuation worksheet (Excel)
3. Draft term sheet for fractional offering (Word)
4. Platform comparison matrix (PDF, 1 page)
5. Final recommendation memo with confidence score (PDF, 2 pages)
6. Recorded recommendation call (audio + transcript)
