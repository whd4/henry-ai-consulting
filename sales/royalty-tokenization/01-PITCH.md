## The problem (in one paragraph)

If you own producing royalty interests, you have a paradox: those interests throw off real cash, but you can't easily sell a fraction. You either sell the whole thing (lose future upside) or hold and hope. The traditional buyers — Sabine Royalty Trust, Texas Pacific Land, the institutional aggregators — pay 60–70 cents on the dollar of net present value because they're the only liquidity in town. **That gap just became a market.**
