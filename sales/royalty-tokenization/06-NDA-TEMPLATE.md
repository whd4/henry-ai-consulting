# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] (the "Effective Date") between:

**HENRY AI Corporation**, a [Texas LLC / Texas Corporation], with principal office in Houston, Texas ("HENRY")

and

**[Client Name / Entity]**, a [State/entity type], with principal office at [Address] ("Client")

(each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss a potential or active engagement related to oil & gas mineral and royalty interest valuation, tokenization advisory, and related strategic services (the "Purpose"). To facilitate that discussion, each Party may disclose certain confidential information to the other.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient") that is identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. This includes, without limitation:

- Production data, lease documents, royalty statements, decimal interests, well-by-well status
- Valuations, financial models, and analytical work product
- Business plans, strategies, customer lists, and operating procedures
- The existence and terms of this engagement and any related discussions
- Any information about ongoing or contemplated tokenization transactions
- Methodology and proprietary HENRY AI infrastructure

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
(a) was publicly available at the time of disclosure;
(b) became publicly available through no breach by Recipient;
(c) was rightfully known to Recipient before disclosure;
(d) was independently developed by Recipient without use of Confidential Information; or
(e) was rightfully received by Recipient from a third party without restriction.

## 3. Obligations

Each Recipient agrees to:
(a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
(b) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, but in no event less than reasonable care;
(c) limit access to Confidential Information to its employees, contractors, and professional advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement; and
(d) not reverse-engineer, disassemble, or decompile any prototypes, software, or tangible objects embodying Confidential Information.

## 4. Required disclosures

If Recipient is required by law, regulation, court order, or governmental authority to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall, to the extent legally permitted, give Discloser prompt written notice and reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order before such disclosure.

## 5. No license

Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, express or implied, in or to any Confidential Information or any intellectual property rights of the Discloser.

## 6. No obligation to engage

This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement, business relationship, or transaction.

## 7. Term

This Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and continues for **two (2) years** thereafter. The confidentiality obligations in Section 3 survive for **five (5) years** following the date of disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information.

## 8. Return or destruction

Within thirty (30) days of written request, Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession and certify such return or destruction in writing. Recipient may retain one archival copy for legal compliance purposes only.

## 9. Equitable relief

The Parties agree that monetary damages may be insufficient for breach of this Agreement and that the non-breaching Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 10. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Exclusive jurisdiction and venue lie in the state and federal courts located in Harris County, Texas.

## 11. Entire agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous communications. Any modification must be in writing signed by both Parties.

## 12. Counterparts and electronic signature

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Electronic signatures (DocuSign, Adobe Sign, scanned PDFs) are binding.
