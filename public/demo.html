Why this matters · in one sentence per stakeholder

For the mineral owner:

You converted a $487K illiquid asset into $487K cash today, while the buyers take all future production risk and reward. Or you can sell 60% and keep 40% upside — your choice.

For the operator:

Same operator, same lease, same RRC filings. Tokenization changes who receives the royalty check — not who operates the well. Zero operational impact.

For the buyer:

Fractional access to a yield-bearing real-world asset, settled in seconds, transparent payout flow, no traditional broker.

For HENRY:

Advisory fee on the structuring (1-3%) + recurring annual platform fee on AUT (0.5-1.5%). Aligned with the asset performing well.
